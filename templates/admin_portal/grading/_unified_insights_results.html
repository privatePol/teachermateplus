What these counts mean

A section has exactly one status within each period: excluded by policy first, then pending academic completion, missing or unsupported measurements, privacy withheld, or eligible. Missing period mappings count as unsupported. The same section can be eligible in one period and exempt in another. Eligible describes that period's mapped assessment pair; movement still needs two compatible completed pairs with the same students across all six measures. How patterns work

Each qualifying section counts once. Meaningful change is at least 3 percentage points, before rounding. Together includes improvement or decline; Opposite means formative indicators and examinations moved in opposite directions; Mixed has another meaningful change; Stable has no meaningful changes. Unavailable sections are outside the percentage denominator.
