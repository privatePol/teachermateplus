Formative–Summative Alignment

Compare independent Quiz, Participation/Output and Examination results. A single assessment pair describes performance; it does not show improvement. All values use saved Gradebook points on supported 0–100 scales.

How movement is classified

Movement compares the same students with all six measures in the latest two mapped pairs. An increase of at least 3 points is improving; a decrease of at least 3 is declining. Smaller changes are stable. Differences use unrounded class averages.

  • Moved Together: all three improved or all three declined.
  • Moved Opposite: Quiz and Participation/Output moved together while Examination moved in the other direction.
  • Mixed Results: another valid combination with at least one meaningful change.
  • Stable Performance: all three changed by less than 3 points. Low performance can also be stable.
