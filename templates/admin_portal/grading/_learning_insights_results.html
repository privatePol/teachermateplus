How section results moved

Improving means a rise of at least {{ threshold }} percentage points; declining means a fall of at least {{ threshold }} percentage points. All smaller changes, including zero, are stable. Quiz classification uses raw attainment percentages, separately from the displayed converted Gradebook quiz points. Moved Together means raw quiz attainment, class standing, and examination averages all improve meaningfully or all decline meaningfully. Moved Opposite means quiz and class standing improve meaningfully while examinations decline meaningfully, or the reverse. Other valid combinations are Mixed / Stable. Unsupported component units are Insufficient Comparable Data and excluded from pattern percentages. Quiz/activity counts are supporting indicators with no minimum-count threshold; these patterns do not establish causation.

Component averages and matched student counts are withheld when small or overlapping student groups could reveal individual grades.
