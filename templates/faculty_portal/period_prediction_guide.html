Simple Explanation
  • EduGrade+ looks at the scores already encoded in this class.
  • It follows the same grading template and grade formula used by the actual gradebook.
  • It then gives an estimate of what grade may happen.
  • It does not save, post, or overwrite any score in the gradebook.

How to Understand the What-If Simulator
  • Choose one student.
  • Type an estimated performance for the still-missing work.
  • The system will answer: "If the student performs around this level, what may happen?"
  • The result is for planning only. It is not an encoded grade and not an approved correction.
  • If a scenario is saved, it is only a private draft note for reference.
Simple example: If you type 80% in Remaining Performance, EduGrade+ is only checking: "If this student gets around 80% on the remaining items, what may the period grade become?" It does not save 80% in the gradebook.
