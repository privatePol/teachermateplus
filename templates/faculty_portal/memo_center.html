Helpful Uses
Short notes work best when they are specific and action-focused.
  • Keep a follow-up note for a student who may be at risk.
  • Write a reminder about a class schedule concern or special rule.
  • Store a private note about make-up work or missing requirements.
  • Pin important notes so they stay visible at the top of the list.
  • Use a class link or student link when you need the memo connected to a specific record.
