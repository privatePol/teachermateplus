Built for Daily Faculty Work

The Faculty Portal is designed around the real teaching workflow, making it easier to manage academic records accurately and efficiently throughout the term.

  • View assigned classes, schedules, and sections
  • Open grading periods for active courses
  • Encode raw scores by activity and component
  • Track attendance with weighted academic rules
  • Review computed grades instantly
  • Monitor grade submission progress
  • Access summary of grades anytime
  • Work within secure and controlled academic rules

Secure, Controlled, and Policy-Driven Grading

EduGradesPro helps institutions enforce grading discipline through controlled access, academic locks, and clearly defined submission rules.

Faculty can encode grades only while the grading period is open.
Closed grading periods are locked from editing.
Submitted grades cannot be changed without authorized reopening.
Reopening actions are logged for monitoring and audit.
Grade computation follows school-defined academic weights.
Access is limited by faculty assignment, campus, and department scope.
