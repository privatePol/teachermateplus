Formal Reference Manual
EduGradesPro Faculty Portal Policies and Governance Manual

This page is written in a more formal tone for academic officers, deans, registrars, and management teams who need a structured reference for workflow, controls, and governance expectations.

1. Purpose and Scope

EduGradesPro Faculty Portal is intended to support assigned faculty members in managing grading activities, attendance, summary review, class list status updates, submissions, and governed corrections for officially assigned classes.

  • The portal is role-aware and limited to faculty-assigned offerings.
  • Its purpose is academic operations, not general student record administration.
  • Institutional governance settings determine deadline behavior, reopen behavior, and correction handling.
2. Role Positioning
Role Primary Responsibility
Faculty Creates activities, encodes scores, records attendance, reviews summaries, submits grades, and requests corrections when needed.
Campus Admin / Dean Reviews governed requests, monitors deadlines, and manages institutional oversight where permissions allow.
Super Admin / Operations Maintains settings, security, permissions, print headers, and system-level governance controls.
3. Faculty Workflow Standard
Stage Expected Action Governance Rationale
Class Selection Faculty opens only assigned offerings in the current academic scope and may review the read-only grading template attached to each class before encoding work. Prevents unauthorized access outside assignment scope.
Activity Setup Faculty creates activities under the proper component, subcomponent, and detail path. Protects grading consistency and reporting accuracy.
Score Encoding Faculty encodes only available scores and leaves missing items blank until ready. If unsaved score entries exist and the faculty tries to leave the score page, EduGradesPro warns that the unsaved encoded data will be lost. Distinguishes unencoded records from zero or actual performance values.
Summary Review Faculty reviews computed outputs before submission. Reduces avoidable correction requests and mis-submissions.
Submission Faculty finalizes only when records are sufficiently complete for the period. Submission is treated as a governance checkpoint, not merely a save action.
Active Period Control When the campus-term active grading period is set, faculty performs normal grading work only in that active period. Other periods remain closed for editing unless they are formally reopened or inside an approved correction window, but Attendance and Summary stay available in read-only mode for review. Prevents premature encoding in future periods while still preserving approved correction access for earlier periods.
Grade Book View History Faculty may open the period-card Who Viewed action to review admin-side read-only grade book monitor openings recorded for that class period. The related grading pages also provide a Quick Jump strip so Activities, Attendance, Summary, and Who Viewed remain reachable with fewer clicks. Improves transparency by letting faculty see when authorized academic monitors opened the grade book.
4. Faculty Assignment Acceptance Governance
Acknowledgment Before Access

Newly assigned classes remain pending until the faculty member accepts the load. Pending assignments block grading and class-list work for that offering.

Faculty Response Options

Faculty may accept, request clarification, or decline the assignment with a written note so the academic office can act on the exact concern.

Response Deadline

Each pending assignment carries a response window. Reminder and expiry behavior follow tenant-configured settings and should be processed by scheduled jobs.

5. Monitoring and Renewal Controls
  • Admin monitoring should treat accepted loads as the operational checkpoint before grading starts.
  • Clarification and decline notes should be reviewed before a load is changed or reissued.
  • Expired assignments require an admin renewal or reissue before the faculty member can respond again.
  • Metric cards in Faculty Assignments should be reviewed for pending, due-within-24-hours, accepted, and expired loads.
  • The class card in Faculty Portal now shows the currently assigned grading template, and faculty may open a read-only template page to review period formulas and class-standing breakdowns before grading.
  • Use manage.py process_faculty_assignment_reminders in scheduled operations so reminders and expirations are applied consistently.
6. Submission Governance
Submission Readiness

Faculty should review active students, missing records, and summary outputs before submission. EduGradesPro surfaces readiness indicators to support this review, and it blocks submission when ACTIVE students still have blank required grade or attendance records.

Readiness Cards and Threshold

The Summary page now presents submission readiness through colored cards for ACTIVE, complete records, missing records, coverage, DRP, WITHDRAWN, and INC. Pass/Fail counts follow the configured passing threshold and normally appear together with the official period grade. EduGradesPro resolves that threshold in this order: Tenant Grading Profile override, assigned grading-template threshold, tenant default setting, then fallback 75.00. Admin may still restrict official-grade release until the configured deadline.

Final Period Grade Trail

The overall FINAL GRADE is shown only on the Final period Summary page. That Final page also displays PRELIM GRADE, MIDTERM GRADE, and PRE-FINAL GRADE columns before the Final Exam so faculty can review the earlier official period results alongside the final-period output.

Final Grade Averaging Rule

EduGradesPro computes the overall FINAL GRADE using the matched tenant grading profile formula for the class. If no special tenant formula is configured, EduGradesPro uses the full configured number of grading periods, so later blank periods still count in the divisor instead of being excluded from the average.

Deadline Enforcement

Submission windows are governed by period lock rules and may be auto-locked based on configured deadlines.

Read-Only Review After Deadline

When a period is no longer open for normal encoding under active-period governance, faculty may still open Attendance and Summary to review stored records, but editing and submission actions stay disabled unless the period is formally reopened or covered by an approved correction window.

Final Clearance

The My Classes page now includes a dedicated Final Clearance action area that lets the faculty member preview campus-term completion status and generate the official final clearance PDF once all clearance conditions are satisfied. Each generated report prints a QR code, Reference No., and Verification Code for NCBA-side validation.

My Signature

When NCBA enables stored signatures, faculty can open My Signature from the portal header to upload an encrypted PNG/JPG signature image. Current-password confirmation is required before the stored signature can be saved, replaced, or removed.

Deadline Reminder Banner

EduGradesPro surfaces the current active grading period and the current grade submission deadline on the Faculty Dashboard, My Classes page, and the class period page so faculty can see both the official working period and its due date before final submission. If a configured deadline belongs to a different campus or term scope, or uses a period code that does not match the class template, the banner now explains that it does not match the faculty member's accepted classes.

Dashboard Priority Actions

The Faculty Dashboard now includes Priority Actions and Students At Risk near the top of the page so faculty can quickly open urgent deadline, missing-grade, score-entry, correction, and intervention follow-ups from one read-only command-center area.

Pinnacle Periodic Grade Summary

The Summary page print button generates an NCBA-branded Summary of Periodic Grades for Pinnacle encoding. The sheet includes the NCBA logo and heading, address, grading period, academic year, semester/term, faculty name, course code, course title, units, section, student list, periodic grade, and status.

Overdue but Still Open

If the submission deadline passes and the class-period is still unfinished, EduGradesPro keeps the grade book open so the faculty member can complete encoding and submit. The period is simply marked overdue and included in the non-compliance monitor for authorized users.

Print Record

The print sheet serves as a formal companion document for review, sign-off, and institutional filing.

7. Grade Corrections and Reopen Governance
System Request Mode

Use the in-portal correction request when institutional policy allows electronic filing and approval tracking.

Manual Only Mode

Use the paper route when the tenant policy requires manual approval and admin reopen processing outside the portal.

After Deadline

If the period is still unsubmitted, continue encoding and submit as soon as possible. If the period was already submitted and later needs changes, use the correction request workflow.

Audit Traceability

Requests, approvals, rejections, reopen actions, gradebook updates, correction attachment uploads/downloads, and optional official correction PDF outputs are captured for accountability and monitoring.

Submission Notifications

When enabled by tenant configuration, filing a correction request can immediately notify selected approver roles such as the college dean and CAO through NCBA-branded email notification cards that include petitioner and period name details.

Direct Score Petition Posting

When the correction petition already contains exact corrected score values, final approval allows EduGradesPro to post the approved values directly into the gradebook, recompute the affected student's submitted period and final grade, and close the request without any additional faculty finalize step.

7.1 Sample Grade Computation

The following example shows how EduGradesPro computes one student's grade using a Base-50 raw-score setup, period component weights, and the final-grade formula. Actual classes follow the grading template assigned to the course.

Base-50 Raw Score Formula

Computed Score = ((Raw Score / Total Score) x 50) + 50

Sample Period Structure

Prelim and Midterm use Class Standing 60% plus Exam 40%. Prefinal uses the class-standing result as the period grade in this example. Final Exam uses the exam score as the period grade.

A. Prelim Sample Inputs
Area Sample Scores Computed Result
Prelim Exam 78/100 89.00
Quizzes Quiz 1: 18/20 = 95.00
Quiz 2: 15/20 = 87.50		 Quiz Average = 91.25
Recitation Recitation 1: 8/10 = 90.00
Recitation 2: 9/10 = 95.00		 Recitation Average = 92.50
Assignment Assignment 1: 18/20 = 95.00
Assignment 2: 16/20 = 90.00		 Assignment Average = 92.50
Activity Activity 1: 28/30 = 96.67
Activity 2: 25/30 = 91.67		 Activity Average = 94.17
B. Prelim Roll-Up
Step Computation Result
Participation / Output (Recitation 92.50 x 40%) + (Assignment 92.50 x 30%) + (Activity 94.17 x 30%) 93.00
Class Standing (Quizzes 91.25 x 40%) + (Participation / Output 93.00 x 60%) 92.30
Prelim Grade (Class Standing 92.30 x 60%) + (Prelim Exam 89.00 x 40%) 90.98
C. Period Results Through Finals
Period Summary of Sample Computation Period Grade
Prelim Detailed above: Class Standing 92.30 and Prelim Exam 89.00 90.98
Midterm Quiz Average 93.75, Participation / Output 92.75, Class Standing 93.15, Midterm Exam 91.00 92.29

8. Grade Prediction Governance
Unofficial and Read-Only

Grade prediction is an advisory tool only. It never writes to the official gradebook and must remain clearly labeled as unofficial.

Same Grading Rules

Prediction uses the same assigned grading template, weighting path, and final-grade formula used by the official computation engine for the class.

Scenario Planning

When enabled, the what-if simulator helps faculty test assumed scores for remaining items in the current grading period without altering saved grading records.

Feature-Governed Access

Access to prediction pages and what-if simulation must be enabled through Configuration Management and can be limited by role.

Interpretation Guidance

The prediction screen should provide a direct link to a separate explanation page that defines each displayed column, explains the methodology and factor set behind each value, documents Base 50 transmutation and the period/final-grade formulas, gives examples, and reminds faculty that simulated values are planning guides only.

Passing-Final Requirement

For students who remain at risk, the prediction screen may also show the average grade still needed across the remaining final periods so faculty can identify the level required to finish with a passing final grade.

Period-Specific Estimate

Each prediction page is tied to one grading period. The main estimated grade is for that current period, while the possible final grade is shown only as secondary planning guidance.

Official Grade Release Control

The Faculty Portal may hide official computed period grades and the official final grade until the configured deadline has passed. This release behavior is governed by admin settings and is separate from the unofficial prediction module.

8.1 Student At-Risk Monitor
Purpose

The at-risk monitor groups students by class and period so faculty can quickly spot who needs follow-up before the period closes.

How to Use

Open the monitor from the Faculty Portal sidebar, then review the students who are currently projected below the passing line.

Next Step

Use the class prediction page or class summary page to inspect coverage, missing items, and the amount still needed to recover.

Governance Note

This is an advisory tool only. It does not replace the official gradebook, submission, or correction process.

9. Student Status Rules
Status Meaning Expected Effect
ACTIVE Student remains part of the graded class list. Included in normal grading coverage and readiness checks.
Removed from Class Student changed schedule and should be removed from this specific class master list without being marked dropped or withdrawn. Excluded from active grading coverage for this class while preserving cleaner distinction from DRP/WITHDRAWN/INC governance. Faculty uses the small remove icon beside the student number and must type remove to confirm the action.
DRP Student is dropped from this particular class. Excluded from active grading coverage and readiness calculations.
WITHDRAWN Student is withdrawn from the term. Excluded from active grading coverage and tracked separately from dropped students.
INC Student has an incomplete class record. Excluded from active grading coverage and readiness calculations until the record is resolved.
The class-list page now also shows a visible Status Legend so faculty can confirm the meaning of ACTIVE, DRP, WITHDRAWN, and INC at a glance before changing any row.
When faculty adds a student to the class, EduGradesPro now supports typed lookup by student number or by the student's last name and first name before the faculty clicks Save.
For easier visual checking, the class list also shows a line number before the student number and formats names as LAST NAME, First Name.
10. Privacy, Security, and Audit Controls
  • Faculty access is limited to assigned offerings and authorized portal functions.
  • Password complexity validation, optional email OTP, forced password change, privacy consent, single-device session enforcement, a 1-hour session timeout, and temporary login lockout are governed centrally.
  • The Faculty Portal quick tour is a read-only orientation aid. Faculty may turn it off for their own next logon using the tour control.
  • Operational actions may be audited, including submission, reopen, correction, and related record changes.
  • Printed or exported records should be handled according to institutional data privacy requirements.
  • The institutional privacy statement remains the authoritative privacy reference.
Official reference: NCBA Data Privacy Notice

12. Faculty Reminder Center and Email Queue
Reminder Center

Faculty may create future activity reminders, due-date reminders, and follow-up notes in one portal location. When a faculty member creates a future-dated activity, EduGradesPro can also auto-create a preparation reminder tied to that activity.

Completion and Snooze

Reminder items can be marked completed or snoozed so the faculty list stays organized and practical.

Optional Email Queue

When tenant configuration enables it, due reminders can queue email notifications to the faculty's registered email address without blocking the production web request.

Performance Safeguard

The reminder email flow is queue-based and batch-processed so the web server does not attempt to send hundreds of emails in a single page request.

Submission Compliance Notices

When tenant configuration enables the non-compliance workflow, the Reminder Center can also show formal overdue-submission notices for a class period. These notices are informational and remain visible until the affected periodic grade book is finally submitted and resolved.

13. Faculty Notes / Private Memo
Private to Faculty

Faculty notes are private working memos for the faculty user and are not visible to students.

Optional Scope

Each memo may remain general or be linked to a specific class or student for easier follow-up.

Pinning and Review

Important notes may be pinned so they stay easy to find, while older notes remain searchable in the memo center.

Recommended Use

Use private memos for intervention reminders, schedule concerns, make-up work notes, and other faculty-only follow-up items.
