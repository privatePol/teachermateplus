Formal Reference Manual
EduGradesPro Faculty Portal Policies and Governance Manual

This page is written in a more formal tone for academic officers, deans, registrars, and management teams who need a structured reference for workflow, controls, and governance expectations.

1. Purpose and Scope

EduGradesPro Faculty Portal is intended to support assigned faculty members in managing grading activities, attendance, summary review, class list status updates, submissions, and governed corrections for officially assigned classes.

  • The portal is role-aware and limited to faculty-assigned offerings.
  • Its purpose is academic operations, not general student record administration.
  • Institutional governance settings determine deadline behavior, reopen behavior, and correction handling.
2. Role Positioning
Role Primary Responsibility
Faculty Creates activities, encodes scores, records attendance, reviews summaries, submits grades, and requests corrections when needed.
Campus Admin / Dean Reviews governed requests, monitors deadlines, and manages institutional oversight where permissions allow.
Super Admin / Operations Maintains settings, security, permissions, print headers, and system-level governance controls.
3. Faculty Workflow Standard
Stage Expected Action Governance Rationale
Class Selection Faculty opens only assigned offerings in the current academic scope and may review the read-only grading template attached to each class before encoding work. Prevents unauthorized access outside assignment scope.
Activity Setup Faculty creates activities under the proper component, subcomponent, and detail path. Protects grading consistency and reporting accuracy.
Score Encoding Faculty encodes only available scores and leaves missing items blank until ready. Distinguishes unencoded records from zero or actual performance values.
Summary Review Faculty reviews computed outputs before submission. Reduces avoidable correction requests and mis-submissions.
Submission Faculty finalizes only when records are sufficiently complete for the period. Submission is treated as a governance checkpoint, not merely a save action.
Active Period Control When the campus-term active grading period is set, faculty performs normal grading work only in that active period. Other periods remain closed unless they are formally reopened or inside an approved correction window. Prevents premature encoding in future periods while still preserving approved correction access for earlier periods.
Grade Book View History Faculty may open the period-card Who Viewed action to review admin-side read-only grade book monitor openings recorded for that class period. Improves transparency by letting faculty see when authorized academic monitors opened the grade book.
4. Faculty Assignment Acceptance Governance
Acknowledgment Before Access

Newly assigned classes remain pending until the faculty member accepts the load. Pending assignments block grading and class-list work for that offering.

Faculty Response Options

Faculty may accept, request clarification, or decline the assignment with a written note so the academic office can act on the exact concern.

Response Deadline

Each pending assignment carries a response window. Reminder and expiry behavior follow tenant-configured settings and should be processed by scheduled jobs.

5. Monitoring and Renewal Controls
  • Admin monitoring should treat accepted loads as the operational checkpoint before grading starts.
  • Clarification and decline notes should be reviewed before a load is changed or reissued.
  • Expired assignments require an admin renewal or reissue before the faculty member can respond again.
  • Metric cards in Faculty Assignments should be reviewed for pending, due-within-24-hours, accepted, and expired loads.
  • The class card in Faculty Portal now shows the currently assigned grading template, and faculty may open a read-only template page to review period formulas and class-standing breakdowns before grading.
  • Use manage.py process_faculty_assignment_reminders in scheduled operations so reminders and expirations are applied consistently.
6. Submission Governance
Submission Readiness

Faculty should review active students, missing records, and summary outputs before submission. EduGradesPro surfaces readiness indicators to support this review.

Deadline Enforcement

Submission windows are governed by period lock rules and may be auto-locked based on configured deadlines.

Deadline Reminder Banner

EduGradesPro surfaces the current active grading period and the current grade submission deadline on the Faculty Dashboard, My Classes page, and the class period page so faculty can see both the official working period and its due date before final submission. If a configured deadline belongs to a different campus or term scope, or uses a period code that does not match the class template, the banner now explains that it does not match the faculty member's accepted classes.

Print Record

The print sheet serves as a formal companion document for review, sign-off, and institutional filing.

7. Grade Corrections and Reopen Governance
System Request Mode

Use the in-portal correction request when institutional policy allows electronic filing and approval tracking.

Manual Only Mode

Use the paper route when the tenant policy requires manual approval and admin reopen processing outside the portal.

Before Deadline

Institutional policy may allow faculty self-reopen before deadline or may require correction workflow review, depending on tenant settings.

After Deadline

Correction and reopen actions become governed requests. Faculty may prepare one request for multiple students or the entire class, and the request can include multiple grading items.

Audit Traceability

Requests, approvals, rejections, reopen actions, gradebook updates, and optional official correction PDF outputs are captured for accountability and monitoring.

Submission Notifications

When enabled by tenant configuration, filing a correction request can immediately notify selected approver roles such as the college dean and CAO through NCBA-branded email notification cards that include petitioner and period name details.

Direct Score Petition Posting

When the correction petition already contains exact corrected score values, final approval allows EduGradesPro to post the approved values directly into the gradebook, recompute the submitted period, and close the request without any additional faculty finalize step.

8. Grade Prediction Governance
Unofficial and Read-Only

Grade prediction is an advisory tool only. It never writes to the official gradebook and must remain clearly labeled as unofficial.

Same Grading Rules

Prediction uses the same assigned grading template, weighting path, and final-grade formula used by the official computation engine for the class.

Scenario Planning

When enabled, the what-if simulator helps faculty test hypothetical remaining scores without altering saved grading records.

Feature-Governed Access

Access to prediction pages and what-if simulation must be enabled through Configurable Features and can be limited by role.

Interpretation Guidance

The prediction screen should provide a direct link to a separate explanation page that defines each displayed column, gives examples, and reminds faculty that simulated values are planning guides only.

Passing-Final Requirement

For students who remain at risk, the prediction screen may also show the average grade still needed across the remaining final periods so faculty can identify the level required to finish with a passing final grade.

8.1 Student At-Risk Monitor
Purpose

The at-risk monitor groups students by class and period so faculty can quickly spot who needs follow-up before the period closes.

How to Use

Open the monitor from the Faculty Portal sidebar, then review the students who are currently projected below the passing line.

Next Step

Use the class prediction page or class summary page to inspect coverage, missing items, and the amount still needed to recover.

Governance Note

This is an advisory tool only. It does not replace the official gradebook, submission, or correction process.

9. Student Status Rules
Status Meaning Expected Effect
ACTIVE Student remains part of the graded class list. Included in normal grading coverage and readiness checks.
Removed from Class Student changed schedule and should be removed from this specific class master list without being marked dropped or withdrawn. Excluded from active grading coverage for this class while preserving cleaner distinction from DR/W governance. Faculty uses the small remove icon beside the student number and must type remove to confirm the action.
DR Student is dropped from this particular class. Excluded from active grading coverage and readiness calculations.
W Student is withdrawn from the term. Excluded from active grading coverage and tracked separately from dropped students.
When faculty adds a student to the class, EduGradesPro now supports typed lookup by student number or by the student's last name and first name before the faculty clicks Save.
For easier visual checking, the class list also shows a line number before the student number and formats names as LAST NAME, First Name.
10. Privacy, Security, and Audit Controls
  • Faculty access is limited to assigned offerings and authorized portal functions.
  • Password complexity validation, forced password change, privacy consent, single-device session enforcement, and temporary login lockout are governed centrally.
  • Operational actions may be audited, including submission, reopen, correction, and related record changes.
  • Printed or exported records should be handled according to institutional data privacy requirements.
  • The institutional privacy statement remains the authoritative privacy reference.
Official reference: NCBA Data Privacy Notice
11. Management Discussion Note

As EduGradesPro governance is finalized, we can continue expanding this manual into a more formal institutional reference with approval matrices, deadline charts, and role-by-role accountability tables.

12. Faculty Reminder Center and Email Queue
Reminder Center

Faculty may create future activity reminders, due-date reminders, and follow-up notes in one portal location. When a faculty member creates a future-dated activity, EduGradesPro can also auto-create a preparation reminder tied to that activity.

Completion and Snooze

Reminder items can be marked completed or snoozed so the faculty list stays organized and practical.

Optional Email Queue

When tenant configuration enables it, due reminders can queue email notifications to the faculty's registered email address without blocking the production web request.

Performance Safeguard

The reminder email flow is queue-based and batch-processed so the web server does not attempt to send hundreds of emails in a single page request.

13. Faculty Notes / Private Memo
Private to Faculty

Faculty notes are private working memos for the faculty user and are not visible to students.

Optional Scope

Each memo may remain general or be linked to a specific class or student for easier follow-up.

Pinning and Review

Important notes may be pinned so they stay easy to find, while older notes remain searchable in the memo center.

Recommended Use

Use private memos for intervention reminders, schedule concerns, make-up work notes, and other faculty-only follow-up items.
