Best Time to Use This Monitor
Use this page as an intervention list, not as an official grade sheet.
Prelim Early warning. Identify students who need attention before the term becomes harder to recover.
Midterm Main intervention period. This is usually the most useful checkpoint for advising and recovery work.
Pre-Final Last intervention checkpoint before final-period outcomes become difficult to change.
Final Use mostly for final review and confirmation. It is usually late for major intervention.
Recommended rhythm: check after Prelim submission, focus heavily during Midterm, and use Pre-Final as the last call for follow-up. The monitor focuses on the current active period when Configuration Management has an active grading period set.
